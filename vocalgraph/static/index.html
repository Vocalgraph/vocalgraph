How the cut-off was chosen

Anything quieter than the cut-off for 0.8 seconds or more is removed. The app measured the recording's background and voice, then tried every cut-off and picked the most aggressive one that removes no audible sound: nothing clearly above the background and within 30 dB of the voice.
