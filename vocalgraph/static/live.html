Vocalgraph

Records from a microphone and shows who is speaking and how each voice sounds, as it happens. When you stop, the full recording is saved to your library and trimmed like any other.

Start a live session

Add a second microphone, or one program's sound (a call, a video, a game): only that program is recorded, not the rest of the computer, and you still hear it as normal. Programs appear once they have made a sound; click Refresh if one is missing. Inputs are mixed for the analysis, and each is also kept as its own track in the saved file.

Pitch, resonance, loudness and breathiness show within about a quarter of a second. Who is speaking follows a moment later (after the first 10 seconds), and colours the measures in. "Replay" plays a saved recording at its normal speed, as if it were live, which is handy for trying this out.
